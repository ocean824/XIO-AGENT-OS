# XIO Product Requirements Document — End-State

## Product promise

A founder can run businesses and build software from one AI-native operating environment with persistent context, trustworthy memory, configurable autonomy, real integrations, and evidence-backed execution.

## Primary user
Ø initially; architecture must support future multi-user/organization productization without rewriting tenant/security foundations.

## Functional domains

### Command / Chat
Universal conversational control, search, navigation, action, approvals, context switching, dashboard widgets and next-best actions.

### Businesses / Workspaces
Multiple companies/projects with isolated/scoped memory, data, permissions, integrations, goals, metrics and operating dashboards.

### Brain
Ingest/retrieve/govern files, notes, emails, meetings, repos, websites, videos/transcripts, databases and generated artifacts. Provenance/confidence required.

### Agents / Skills / Workflows
Create/configure agents; assign roles/models/tools; reusable skills; scheduled/event workflows; approvals; run history; costs; evals.

### FORGE
Full native software lifecycle described in `FORGE_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

### Comms / Calendar
Unified communication and meetings with summaries, drafting, sending under approval, follow-up and memory.

### Projects / Tasks
Business and software project management, dependencies, goals, milestones, evidence and agent assignment.

### Data
Spreadsheet, SQL, ETL/data pipelines, charts, structured extraction, recurring jobs, imports/exports and lineage.

### CRM / Growth
Contacts/companies, relationship graph, deals, investor/customer segmentation, campaigns, outreach, funnel stages and analytics.

### Funnel / Web
Generate/edit/deploy landing pages, funnels, forms, offers and experiments; export source; connect analytics/domains/payments.

### Social / Content / Ads
Content pipeline, assets, calendar, publishing adapters, analytics, ad planning/management where authorized, attribution and experiments.

### Studio
Documents, proposals, reports, decks, animated HTML presentations, spreadsheets, creative assets and brand templates.

### Finance
Multi-business revenue/expense/cash-flow, invoices/subscriptions, budgets/scenarios, alerts and connectors.

### Intel
Personalized news/research/watchlists/competitive intelligence with provenance, change detection and actions.

### Integrations
Connector center, OAuth/secrets, health/status, scopes, syncs, webhooks, MCP/API/plugin SDK.

## Non-functional
- production-grade security/audit;
- model/provider portability;
- graceful degradation;
- accessible responsive UI;
- desktop-first deep work + mobile companion;
- observable/recoverable services;
- scalable background execution;
- deterministic calculations/rules where appropriate;
- no fake integration states;
- source-backed AI outputs for factual business intelligence;
- explicit human control over consequential actions.

## Success criteria

XIO succeeds when Ø can open one environment and reliably answer/act on: what needs attention; what changed; what agents are doing; what money/deals/projects are doing; what communications need response; what content/campaigns are working; what software is being built; what failed; what evidence proves completion; and what XIO recommends doing next—without manually reconstructing context across dozens of tools.
